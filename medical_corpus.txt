Standard Medical Laboratory Reference Ranges and Guidance Rules:

1. Total Cholesterol: Normal is less than 200 mg/dL. Borderline high is 200 to 239 mg/dL. High cholesterol is 240 mg/dL and above. High cholesterol can lead to plaque buildup in arteries.
2. LDL Cholesterol (Bad Cholesterol): Optimal is less than 100 mg/dL. Near optimal is 100-129 mg/dL. High LDL is 160 to 189 mg/dL.
3. HDL Cholesterol (Good Cholesterol): Protective level is 60 mg/dL and above. Low HDL is less than 40 mg/dL for men and less than 50 mg/dL for women.
4. Triglycerides: Normal is less than 150 mg/dL. Borderline high is 150-199 mg/dL. High is 200 to 499 mg/dL.
5. Fasting Blood Glucose: Normal fasting level is 70 to 99 mg/dL. Prediabetes is 100 to 125 mg/dL. Diabetes threshold is 126 mg/dL or higher on two separate tests.
6. Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c): Normal is below 5.7%. Prediabetes range is 5.7% to 6.4%. Diabetes indicator is 6.5% or higher.
7. Hemoglobin (CBC): Normal reference range for adult males is 13.8 to 17.2 g/dL. Normal for adult females is 12.1 to 15.1 g/dL. Low hemoglobin indicates potential anemia.
8. White Blood Cell Count (WBC): Normal reference range is 4500 to 11000 cells/mcL. Elevated WBC (leukocytosis) indicates possible infection, inflammation, or physical stress.
9. Platelet Count: Normal reference range is 150000 to 450000 platelets/mcL. Low platelets (thrombocytopenia) increases bleeding risk.
10. Serum Creatinine (Kidney Function): Normal for males is 0.74 to 1.35 mg/dL. Normal for females is 0.59 to 1.04 mg/dL. Elevated creatinine suggests impaired kidney filtration.
11. Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN): Normal range is 7 to 20 mg/dL. High BUN indicates reduced renal function or dehydration.
12. Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT - Liver Function): Normal range is 7 to 56 units per liter (U/L). Elevated ALT suggests liver cell inflammation or stress.
13. Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST): Normal range is 10 to 40 U/L. Elevated AST indicates liver, heart, or muscle stress.
14. Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH): Normal range is 0.4 to 4.0 mIU/L. High TSH suggests hypothyroidism (underactive thyroid). Low TSH suggests hyperthyroidism.
15. Maximum Exercise Heart Rate: Normal target is estimated as 220 minus age. During exercise testing, reaching 70% to 85% of max heart rate is typical. Failing to reach 85% may indicate chronotropic incompetence.
16. Resting Heart Rate: Normal adult resting heart rate is 60 to 100 beats per minute (bpm). Tachycardia is over 100 bpm resting; bradycardia is below 60 bpm.
17. Dietary Fiber & Lipid Guidance: Patients with elevated cholesterol should increase soluble fiber intake (oats, legumes, fruit) to reduce cholesterol absorption.
18. Physical Activity Guidance: Adults are recommended 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise per week.
19. Sodium Restriction Rule: Individuals with elevated cardiovascular risk or hypertension should limit daily sodium intake to less than 2300 mg per day.
20. Clinical Non-Diagnostic Rule: All lab explanations provided to patients must be purely educational and non-diagnostic, strictly recommending direct consultation with a qualified primary care physician for clinical diagnosis and treatment planning.
